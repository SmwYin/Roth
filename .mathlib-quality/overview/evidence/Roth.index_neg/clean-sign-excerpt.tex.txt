    = \sum_{\sigma \in S_l} \signature(\sigma) \prod_{\nu=0}^{l-1} \Delta_{\sigma(\nu+1)-1} \frac{1}{\nu !} \frac{\partial^\nu}{\partial x_p^\nu} R.
