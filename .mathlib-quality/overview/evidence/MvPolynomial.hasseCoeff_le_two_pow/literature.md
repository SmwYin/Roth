Standard finite-set form inferred from the cited Hasse and scalar binomial formulas, rather than identified as an exact named theorem in these sources: finite S and natural families k,d,r with k_i<=r_i on S give product binom(k_i,d_i)<=2^(sum r_i). For the support-defined Hasse factor take S=support(d); the ambient type can be arbitrary. The global finite-type box estimate is a corollary. No polynomial coefficients, characteristic-zero assumption, normed ring or factorial division is needed. No historical change in this elementary inequality was evidenced.
