1140: Let $\alpha$ be an irrational real algebraic number and let $\kappa\in\R$. Suppose there are infinitely many pairs of integers $h,q$ with $q>0$ such that
1141: \begin{equation}\label{roth:eq1}
1142: \left| \alpha - \frac{h}{q}\right| <\frac{1}{q^\kappa}.
1143: \end{equation}
1144: \begin{thm}[Roth]
1145:     Under the above conditions, $\kappa \leq 2$.
1146: \end{thm}
1147: \begin{lem}
1148:     If the theorem fails, then it also fails for an irrational real algebraic integer.
1149: \end{lem}
1150: \begin{proof}
1151: Suppose the theorem fails for a real irrational algebraic number $\alpha$: there is a $\kappa>2$ such that Equation \eqref{roth:eq1} holds for infinitely many integer pairs $h,q$ with $q>0$.
1152: 
1153: \begin{lem}
1154:     If $\alpha$ is an algebraic number, there is an integer $M>0$ such that $M\alpha$ is an algebraic integer.
1155: \end{lem}
1156: \begin{proof}
1157:     Clear denominators in a nonzero rational polynomial annihilating
1158:     $\alpha$ and change its sign so that its leading coefficient is a
1159:     positive integer $M$. Write it as
1160:     $MX^d+b_{d-1}X^{d-1}+\cdots+b_0$, where $d\geq1$.
1161:     Then $\beta=M\alpha$ satisfies the monic integral equation
1162:     \[
1163:     \beta^d+\sum_{j=0}^{d-1}b_jM^{d-1-j}\beta^j=0.
1164:     \]
1165:     Thus $M\alpha$ is an algebraic integer.
1166: \end{proof}
1167: Let $\alpha' = M \alpha$. Then multiplying Equation \eqref{roth:eq1} by $M$ gives
1168: $$
1169: \left| \alpha' - \frac{h'}{q}\right| <\frac{M}{q^\kappa}
1170: $$
1171: with $h' = Mh$. Pick any $\kappa'$ such that $2 < \kappa' < \kappa$. Then for $q$ large enough,
1172: $$
1173: \frac{M}{q^\kappa} < \frac{1}{q^{\kappa'}}.
1174: $$
1175: For a fixed upper bound on $q$, there are only finitely many possible
1176: integers $h$ satisfying the approximation inequality. The infinitely many
1177: original solutions therefore have unbounded denominators, and discarding
1178: bounded $q$ leaves infinitely many. For such $q$ we have
1179: $$
1180: \left| \alpha' - \frac{h'}{q}\right| <\frac{1}{q^{\kappa'}}.
1181: $$
1182: The algebraic integer $\alpha'$ is still irrational, because $M$ is a
1183: nonzero integer. It has infinitely many approximations with exponent
1184: $\kappa'>2$, as required.
1185: \end{proof}
