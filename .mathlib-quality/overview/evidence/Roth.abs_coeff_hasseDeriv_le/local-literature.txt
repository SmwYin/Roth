438: \begin{lem}\label{roth:lem3}
439: Let $R$ and $F$ be as above, and suppose all the coefficients of $R$ have absolute value bounded by $B$. Then all the coefficients   of $F$ have absolute value bounded by
440: $$
441: ((r_1 + 1) \cdots (r_p+1))^l l! B^l 2^{(r_1 + \dots + r_p)l}.
442: $$
443: \end{lem}
444: \begin{proof}
445: For any polynomial $f=\sum_e a_eX^e$, put
446: \[
447: abc(f)=\max_e|a_e|\quad(abc(0)=0),\qquad
448: \|f\|_1=\sum_e|a_e|.
449: \]
450: We prove the stronger bound for $\|F\|_1$. The triangle inequality gives
451: $\|f+kg\|_1\leq\|f\|_1+|k|\|g\|_1$. For monomials (including the zero
452: polynomial), $\|f_1\cdots f_l\|_1=\prod_\nu\|f_\nu\|_1$.
453: 
454: Set $S=r_1+\cdots+r_p$ and $N=\prod_j(r_j+1)$. Pad the monomial expansion
455: of $R$ by zero terms and write $R=\sum_{i=1}^N\alpha_i$, each
456: $\alpha_i=u_iX^{s(i)}$ with $|u_i|\leq B$ and $s(i)_j\leq r_j$.
457: An individual Hasse derivative of such a term is zero or a monomial whose
458: coefficient has absolute value at most
459: \[
460: |u_i|\prod_j{s(i)_j\choose d_j}\leq B2^S.
461: \]
462: This applies to $\Delta_\mu\Delta^{(p)}_\nu\alpha_i$, where
463: $\Delta^{(p)}_\nu=(1/\nu!)\partial_{x_p}^{\nu}$, since $\Delta_\mu$ uses
464: only the first $p-1$ variables.
465: 
466: Let $\mathcal X$ be the set of maps $\{0,\dots,l-1\}\to\{1,\dots,N\}$.
467: Multilinearity in the columns gives
468: \[
469: F=\sum_{f\in\mathcal X}
470:  \det\bigl(\Delta_\mu\Delta^{(p)}_\nu\alpha_{f(\nu)}\bigr)_{\mu,\nu=0}^{l-1}.
471: \]
472: Write $S_l$ for the permutations of $\{1,\dots,l\}$.
473: For $\sigma\in S_l$, the map $\nu\mapsto\sigma(\nu+1)-1$ permutes
474: $\{0,\dots,l-1\}$, matching the row and column indices of the matrix.
475: The signs have absolute value $1$. Thus, for each $f$,
476: \begin{align*}
477: \left\|\det\bigl(\Delta_\mu\Delta^{(p)}_\nu\alpha_{f(\nu)}\bigr)\right\|_1
478: &\leq\sum_{\sigma\in S_l}
479:  \left\|\prod_{\nu=0}^{l-1}
480:       \Delta_{\sigma(\nu+1)-1}\Delta^{(p)}_\nu\alpha_{f(\nu)}\right\|_1\\
481: &=\sum_{\sigma\in S_l}\prod_{\nu=0}^{l-1}
482:  \left\|\Delta_{\sigma(\nu+1)-1}\Delta^{(p)}_\nu\alpha_{f(\nu)}\right\|_1\\
483: &\leq l!(B2^S)^l.
484: \end{align*}
485: There are $N^l$ maps in $\mathcal X$. Hence
486: \[
487: abc(F)\leq\|F\|_1\leq N^ll!(B2^S)^l,
488: \]
489: which proves both the assertion and the promised stronger estimate.
490: \end{proof}
