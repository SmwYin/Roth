The standard coordinate identity is n!Δ_(ne_j)=∂_j^n; the standard abstract analogue is factorial scaling for an **iterative** family. Arbitrary Hasse–Schmidt derivations need not be iterative. Over Q-algebras factorials can be divided out; in characteristic p, higher Hasse operators carry information that ordinary iterates lose. The source semiring formulation avoids unnecessary characteristic, domain, finiteness and invertibility assumptions. A generic additive recurrence is a real proof abstraction and is checked separately, not claimed as existing literature's full Hasse–Schmidt structure.
