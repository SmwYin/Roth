The identified object is the Hasse/divided-derivative Wronskian matrix, equivalently a matrix of truncated polynomial jets before evaluation. Sources agree on the row-order construction but vary in normalization (ordinary vs divided derivatives), allowed derivative orders and base ring. Only the normalized form matches Roth exactly. Fields of characteristic zero identify the forms after factorial row scaling; they are not definitionally equal. The primary source's field setting concerns linear independence criteria, which must not be transferred automatically to arbitrary semirings or positive characteristic.
