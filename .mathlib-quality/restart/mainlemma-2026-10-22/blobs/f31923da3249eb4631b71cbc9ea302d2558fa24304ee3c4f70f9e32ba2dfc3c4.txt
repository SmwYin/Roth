## Mathematics before golf

For S=∑j rj, differentiation by k preserves each coordinate degree and bounds each coefficient by 2^S B. A further derivative i preserves degree again and bounds coefficients by 2^S(2^S B). Evaluating on the diagonal at α multiplies by at most ∏j(1+|α|)^rj=(1+|α|)^S. Since S≤s and both bases are at least1, the original bound is at most 2^s(2^s B)(1+|α|)^s. The two strict upper bounds by β and B>0 yield the advertised strict β(βB)β. No irrationality, algebraicity, nonzero P, nonempty variables, positive s, positive rj, or sign assumption on α is needed.

Source clean.tex1537–1559 separately estimates Q coefficients, its further derivative, and evaluation. The helper represents exactly that final mathematical stage and is more general than Fin m. It does not encapsulate counting, common witnesses, index, or Lemma7. No cost-hiding permission follows from its private visibility.
